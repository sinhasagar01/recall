Three lines, because three is what a day is

One explanation, one rebuild, one applied decision — your own definition of a day, typed by hand each morning. Not derived from a phase, not suggested, not scheduled. The app does not know what you should do today and will not pretend to; it holds what you decided, so you stop holding it.

The only derived thing on this page is the practice line, and every number on it links through to the thing it counts. No hours, no percentage, no streak, no calendar, no heatmap. "1 / 3" in the rail is a count of boxes you ticked yourself.

A log, and deliberately not a chart

No calendar grid, no heatmap, no streak, no completion rate over time. The one thing worth looking back for is a blocker — "what was I stuck on last Tuesday, and did I ever resolve it?" — and the way to answer that is to read the words. A grid of green squares answers a different question, one this product has refused since the first spec.

Days with nothing typed do not appear at all. Thursday shows because one line was written and none ticked, which is a real day; a day you never opened the app is not a record of anything and gets no row.

The rules

Every decision this reference encodes. Where this page and the screens disagree, the screens win.

Data
  • One new table, daysOne row per date per user, unique on (user_id, day). Three intention texts and three done flags, plus the blocker text and its resolved timestamp. Fifth table in five arcs, and the last one.
  • A day is keyed by a date, not a timestampThe user's local date, computed the way localDateString already computes it for evidence — never toISOString, which gives yesterday or tomorrow depending on the hour.
  • Nothing links to anythingThe three lines are free text. No source, no capability, no ledger item, no topic. Linking them would make Today derive its content, which is the decision already taken against.
  • A day with nothing typed has no rowOpening the app does not create a record. A day you never wrote on is not a day you failed.
Behaviour
  • You type the three, every morningNot derived from a phase, not suggested, not scheduled. The app does not know what you should do today and will not pretend to.
  • Ticking your own intention is not ticking your own competenceArc 3 refuses a manual tick because a capability is a claim about ability. A daily intention is a claim about what you did, and only you can make it. Different kinds of claim, different rules — state this in DESIGN.md, because the two boxes look identical and the distinction is the whole reason one is clickable.
  • Yesterday's unticked lines are prefilled, not carriedThey arrive as editable text with a clear button. Automatic carry-forward is how a to-do list grows a backlog you stop reading.
  • A blocker outlives its dayIt shows on Today until you mark it resolved. Resolving keeps the text and stamps the date. A problem written on Friday and forgotten by Monday is what the field exists to prevent.
  • Earlier days are read-onlyTicking Thursday's box from Sunday's chair is writing history rather than recording it.
  • Days with no row do not appear in the logNo gaps to explain, no empty rows implying failure.
The one derived thing
  • No carry badge — correctedAn earlier draft of this page drew carried · 2 days on an unticked line. It is dropped. Producing that count means walking back through days comparing text, which is a second derived thing and the multi-day scan the blocker rule avoids — and there is no lineage stored to count, because lines are prefilled, not carried. The Clear button and its hint already say where the text came from. Five references running have now contradicted a shipped rule or their own brief; see TASKS.md for the two shapes it keeps taking.
  • The practice line, and nothing elseQueue count, review count, longest gap — each links through to the thing it counts. The product review's rule holds: if a number here cannot be clicked through to what it counts, it does not belong here.
  • No weak topic list on TodayThat is the weak page's job and duplicating it here is how this page becomes a dashboard.
Not built
  • No calendar, heatmap, streak or completion rateA log you read, not a chart you look at. The one reason to look back is a blocker, and reading the words is how you answer that.
  • No reminders, no notifications, no scheduling1–3–7–21 runs manually outside the app by decision.
  • Today is not the landing pageThe library stays the front door. This is a learning library, and opening it on a to-do list would change what the product is on the first screen you see every day.
  • No AIArc 6 attaches elsewhere. Nothing stubbed here.
