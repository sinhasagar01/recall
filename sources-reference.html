The one number this screen exists to show

Not how many videos you saved — how many turned into something you can recall. The dots are the five extractions, not progress through the video: the app has no idea how much of a video you watched and must not pretend to. A source with nothing after two weeks turns crimson, which is the honest sentence about six hours you will not get back.

Source rows are deliberately not topic cards — no serif title, no confidence meter, no left accent rule. A source is raw material, and the moment it looks like a topic it competes with your library for the same attention. Sources sit under their own rail group, below the three that matter.

The one thing that must never be prefilled

Selecting transcript text fills the definition. It never fills the mental model. The definition is the fact and someone else's words are fine for it; the mental model is the correction only you can write, and a prefilled one would be a quotation pretending to be understanding. That single rule is the difference between this and a note-taking app.

Why var in a loop surprises everyone once

JavaScriptclosuresgotchas·Medium
Definition

A var declaration in a loop creates one binding shared by every iteration, so every closure created inside sees the same final value.

Mental model

One backpack passed around the whole loop, not one backpack each. Everybody ends up holding whatever was last put in it.

The rules

Every decision this reference encodes. Where this page and the screens disagree, the screens win.

Data
  • One new table, sourcesTitle required; course, URL and transcript optional. Plus a nullable source_id on topics. This is the first new entity since the schema was built, and it is one because a source is genuinely not a topic — it has no confidence, is never practised, and dies without taking its children with it.
  • The transcript is scratchDeletable, and the delete is not recoverable. The source record — title, course, URL — survives it. A source with no transcript is a normal, supported state, not a broken one.
  • The transcript is never in library searchA transcript matching “closure” would bury the topic you wrote about closures under the paragraph that taught you it. It is searchable only inside its own workspace.
  • Deleting a source keeps its topicson delete set null. The entries stay; they lose the line saying where they came from. The confirmation says so.
The five extractions
  • Four are derived, one is tickedDefinition and mental model come from linked topics; the challenge is derived from arc 1's challenge evidence on any linked topic; the retrieval questions from a count of linked quizzes. Only “when not to use it” is a manual tick, because nothing in the data distinguishes that topic from any other.
  • Derived means it cannot be gamedYou cannot tick “three retrieval questions” with two quizzes written, in the same way you cannot tick a capability by finishing a video. The one manual item is the exception and is labelled as one.
Behaviour
  • Selecting fills the definition, never the mental modelSomeone else's words are fine for the fact. The model is the correction only you can write.
  • The dots count extractions, not video progressThe app does not know how much of a video you watched and must not pretend to.
  • Nothing distilled after fourteen days turns crimson — on the LABEL onlyThe one place this product says something uncomfortable, and it should. But an earlier draft of this page argued for it without checking the token rule: DESIGN.md reserves --flag for weak confidence, a destructive action, or an error, and a stale source is a fourth thing. Corrected: the yield label “Nothing · 15 days” takes the colour and the row stays neutral. A crimson row would read as an error, and a stale source is not an error — it is a fact about me. DESIGN.md carries the exception, worded that narrowly.
  • A source row is not a topic cardNo serif title, no confidence meter, no accent rule. Raw material must not compete with the library.
  • Mobile stacks and collapses the transcriptSelect-to-fill is desktop only; on a touch screen you read and type.
  • Sources is reached from the account surface on mobile — correctedAn earlier draft of this page drew the mobile screens without showing how you get to them, which left the route to be assumed. The tab bar stays two destinations plus add (DESIGN.md rule 13, cited by name in the app layout): Sources sits beside Settings in the account surface, the same precedent Settings itself set rather than becoming a fourth nav entry. A source is somewhere you go deliberately, not somewhere you bounce between, and a fourth tap target plus the FAB across 390pt is how a tab bar stops being usable.

    One further correction, found while building: the account foot in the desktop rail is not that surface. The whole rail is hidden below 860px, so a link placed in its foot renders at no width at all. The mobile account surface is the md:hidden cluster in the library head — where Settings, Sign out and Delete account already live — and that is where Sources goes. The first frame on the Mobile tab draws it, so the route in is visible rather than assumed.
Where arc 6 attaches — designed for, not built
  • Two AI actions will live in the workspaceFind what I missed beside the extraction checklist, and draft retrieval questions beside the quiz mode of the distil panel. Leave room for a single action button in each panel head; do not draw them, do not stub them, and do not add a disabled control. A ghost button is worse than no button.
  • The transcript must stay reachable server-sideBoth actions send it to a model from the server. Do not design a flow where the transcript exists only in browser state, and do not delete-on-distil automatically — the delete stays a deliberate act.
Not built
  • No AI in this arcNo summarisation, no extraction, no generated questions. Distillation is manual and the UI's job is to make manual fast.
  • No scheduling, no dashboard, no analytics, no gamificationInherited unchanged.
  • No source-level confidence, no source practiceA transcript is a document and is never practised.
  • No file upload, no video embedding, no auto-fetched transcriptsYou paste text.
