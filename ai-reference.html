The rules

Every decision this reference encodes. Where this page and the screens disagree, the screens win.

What the model does
  • It extracts what the video taughtTitle, definition, the instructor's mental model, a "when not to use it", and two or three questions with distractors — per concept, for every concept in the transcript. This is transcription of teaching, not invention.
  • The mental model is the instructor's, and editableThese courses give one. Extracting it is the same act as extracting the definition — someone else's words, correctly attributed to the source. Replace it when you have a better one, in review or later on the topic page.
  • It never grades youNo score, no confidence written back, no judgement of your recall. Confidence stays the one number in this app that is yours.
Review is not optional
  • Nothing is saved until you press SaveExtraction produces a review screen and nothing else. No topic exists until you have looked at the list.
  • Kept by default, duplicates untickedYou are mining a video; unticking two beats ticking twelve. A concept matching something already in your library arrives unticked with the reason shown.
  • Every field is editable in reviewTitle, definition, mental model, when-not-to-use, and every question and distractor. Dropping a single question is one action.
Data
  • Saved concepts are ordinary topics and quizzesSame table, same confidence, same practice queue, linked to the source through arc 2's source_id. Nothing about practice, weak topics or export changes.
  • One flag: extracted or writtenA boolean on topics, shown only as a library filter chip. It answers "which of these did I actually think about" months from now, and costs one column and one chip.
  • Coverage is stored; model prose is notThe coverage list — what the video contained, what you kept, what you dropped and why — lives on sources, because it is the record of a decision. Nothing else from a model call is persisted.
  • The key is server-side onlyOPENAI_API_KEY, never NEXT_PUBLIC_ prefixed, held by a source-level assertion modelled on secret-key-boundary.test.ts. The browser never sees it and never talks to OpenAI.
  • Guard tokens must not be ordinary wordsArc 5 shipped a guard matching days in prose about practice gaps. ai occurs inside said and again; model occurs in "mental model" on every page. Corrected: openai. is not a usable token either — it already occurs twice in supabase/config.toml, and it matches platform.openai.com, which this reference's own out-of-credit copy contains. The guard names OPENAI_API_KEY (scoped to src/, since config.toml holds it too), api.openai.com, and NEXT_PUBLIC_OPENAI as a separate rule.
Cost and consent
  • Every call is opt-in, per actionNothing on page load, nothing on save, nothing in the background. No automatic extraction, no extract-on-paste.
  • The send box names what leaves, before it leavesThe transcript, its size, the token estimate, the cost, and how long it will take. A transcript too long for one pass says so before you press, not after you are billed.
  • Cancel stops waiting, and says soA server action is one round trip with no client-reachable abort, so the call finishes on the server. And nothing would make the original claim true: tokens already generated are billed either way. The copy is “Cancelling stops waiting. The call may still finish, and you may still be billed for it.”
  • No key means no featureThe manual path is arc 2 unchanged, and it stays the fallback rather than becoming vestigial.
What retiring the exclusion does and does not do
  • Only the no-AI exclusion is retiredNo scheduling, no dashboard, no analytics, no gamification, no streaks, no notifications, no sharing, no spaced repetition. One line retired, the rest inherited.
  • It is a recorded reversalDESIGN.md and ARCHITECTURE.md state that the exclusion existed, why, what changed — these courses supply mental models, so extracting one is not authoring one — and what replaced it.
  • The honest risk, recorded rather than designed aroundA library of extracted topics is one you have read rather than written, and recognition can read as knowledge when quizzed. The extracted/written filter exists so that is visible. Nothing else guards it, and nothing else should.
